---
title: Inexact Newton bundle adjustment computes the reduced camera system correctly and every truncated step is safe
proves: [correctness]
status: full
---

We use the notation of the page and of the sparse bundle adjustment page. The parameters are ordered as the free cameras (6 each) and then the points (3 each). $J$ is the Jacobian of the residual at the current parameters with respect to the local update, $g = J^{\mathsf T} r$, and
$$J^{\mathsf T} J = \begin{pmatrix} U & W \\ W^{\mathsf T} & V \end{pmatrix}, \qquad U^* = U + \mu I, \quad V^* = V + \mu I, \quad \mu > 0.$$
$U$ and $V$ are block diagonal, with $6 \times 6$ blocks $U_i$ and $3 \times 3$ blocks $V_j$, and $W$ has the $6 \times 3$ block $W_{ij}$ when camera $i$ sees point $j$. The reduced camera matrix is $S = U^* - W V^{*-1} W^{\mathsf T}$, and the right side is $e = -g_a + W V^{*-1} g_b$.

**Claim.** (a) `apply` returns $S x$ and `rhs` returns $e$. (b) $S$ and its diagonal blocks are symmetric positive definite, so the block Jacobi preconditioner of line L6 exists and is positive definite, and in exact arithmetic PCG reaches the exact solution in at most $6N_f$ iterations, where $N_f$ is the number of free cameras. (c) Let $\delta = (\delta_a, \delta_b)$, where $\delta_a$ is the PCG iterate after any number $k \ge 1$ of iterations (or $k = 0$ when $e = 0$) and $\delta_b$ comes from line L8. Then the damped model $m_\mu(\delta) = g^{\mathsf T} \delta + \tfrac12 \delta^{\mathsf T} (J^{\mathsf T} J + \mu I) \delta$ satisfies $m_\mu(\delta) \le 0$, and if $g \ne 0$ then $m_\mu(\delta) < 0$ and the predicted reduction $\operatorname{pred} = -g^{\mathsf T} \delta - \tfrac12 \|J \delta\|^2$ computed in line L10 is positive. (d) Accepted steps lower $F$ strictly, and at a point with $g \ne 0$ only finitely many steps are rejected before one is accepted.

**Proof of (a).** For a vector $x$ over the free cameras, $(W^{\mathsf T} x)_j = \sum_{i \text{ sees } j} W_{ij}^{\mathsf T} x_i$, then $z_j = V_j^{*-1} (W^{\mathsf T} x)_j$ because $V^*$ is block diagonal, and $(W z)_i = \sum_{j \text{ seen by } i} W_{ij} z_j$. The code computes $z_j$ for each point from the cameras that see it, and subtracts $W_{ij} z_j$ from the block of camera $i$ for each such camera. Together with $U_i^* x_i$ this is $U^* x - W V^{*-1} W^{\mathsf T} x = S x$. The same argument with $x$ replaced by $g_b$ in the point space gives $e$. $\square$

**Proof of (b).** $M = J^{\mathsf T} J + \mu I$ is positive definite because $v^{\mathsf T} M v = \|J v\|^2 + \mu \|v\|^2$. Each $V_j^*$ is a diagonal block of $M$, so it is positive definite and invertible. For $a \ne 0$ put $b = -V^{*-1} W^{\mathsf T} a$; then $(a, b)^{\mathsf T} M (a, b) = a^{\mathsf T} S a$, as on the sparse bundle adjustment page, so $S$ is positive definite. A diagonal block $S_{ii} = E_i^{\mathsf T} S E_i$, with $E_i$ the $6$ columns of camera $i$ in the identity, is positive definite too, and so is its inverse. So the preconditioner $P = \operatorname{diag}(S_{ii}^{-1})$ is positive definite. PCG with a symmetric positive definite matrix $S$ and preconditioner $P$ is the conjugate gradient method applied to $L^{\mathsf T} S L$ with $P = L L^{\mathsf T}$, and conjugate gradients on a positive definite matrix of order $6N_f$ reach the exact solution in at most $6N_f$ iterations in exact arithmetic (Nocedal and Wright, Theorem 5.1 and Section 5.1). The check $p^{\mathsf T} S p \le 0$ in `pcg` therefore never fires in exact arithmetic. $\square$

**Proof of (c).** Fix $\delta_a$ and minimize $m_\mu(\delta_a, \delta_b)$ over $\delta_b$. The terms with $\delta_b$ are $g_b^{\mathsf T} \delta_b + \delta_a^{\mathsf T} W \delta_b + \tfrac12 \delta_b^{\mathsf T} V^* \delta_b$, a strictly convex quadratic whose minimizer is $\delta_b = -V^{*-1} (g_b + W^{\mathsf T} \delta_a)$. This is line L8. Putting it back gives
$$m_\mu(\delta_a, \delta_b) = q(\delta_a) := \tfrac12 \delta_a^{\mathsf T} S \delta_a - e^{\mathsf T} \delta_a - \tfrac12 g_b^{\mathsf T} V^{*-1} g_b,$$
which one checks by expanding: the cross terms collect to $-\tfrac12 (g_b + W^{\mathsf T} \delta_a)^{\mathsf T} V^{*-1} (g_b + W^{\mathsf T} \delta_a)$, and $\delta_a^{\mathsf T} W V^{*-1} W^{\mathsf T} \delta_a$ together with $\tfrac12 \delta_a^{\mathsf T} U^* \delta_a$ gives $\tfrac12 \delta_a^{\mathsf T} S \delta_a$, while $g_a^{\mathsf T} \delta_a - \delta_a^{\mathsf T} W V^{*-1} g_b = -e^{\mathsf T} \delta_a$.

PCG started at $0$ produces iterates $x_k$ that minimize $q$ over the nested affine sets $\mathcal{K}_k = \operatorname{span}\{P e, (P S) P e, \dots, (P S)^{k-1} P e\}$ (the minimization property of conjugate gradients, applied to the transformed system; Nocedal and Wright, Theorem 5.2). Since $0 \in \mathcal{K}_k$, $q(x_k) \le q(0) = -\tfrac12 g_b^{\mathsf T} V^{*-1} g_b \le 0$. If $e \ne 0$ and $k \ge 1$, the first iterate is $x_1 = \alpha_0 P e$ with $\alpha_0 = e^{\mathsf T} P e / (e^{\mathsf T} P S P e) > 0$, and $q(x_1) - q(0) = -\tfrac12 (e^{\mathsf T} P e)^2 / (e^{\mathsf T} P S P e) < 0$, so then $q(x_k) < q(0)$.

So $m_\mu(\delta) \le 0$. Now let $g \ne 0$. If $g_b \ne 0$, then $q(0) < 0$ because $V^{*-1}$ is positive definite. If $g_b = 0$, then $g_a \ne 0$ and $e = -g_a \ne 0$, and $q(x_k) < q(0) = 0$ by the last remark. In both cases $m_\mu(\delta) < 0$, so $\delta \ne 0$. Finally
$$\operatorname{pred} = -g^{\mathsf T} \delta - \tfrac12 \|J \delta\|^2 = -m_\mu(\delta) + \tfrac12 \mu \|\delta\|^2 > 0.$$
The code computes $\|J \delta\|^2 = \delta_a^{\mathsf T} U \delta_a + 2 \delta_a^{\mathsf T} W \delta_b + \delta_b^{\mathsf T} V \delta_b$ from the blocks, which is $\delta^{\mathsf T} J^{\mathsf T} J \delta$. $\square$

Note that the formula $\tfrac12 \delta^{\mathsf T} (\mu \delta - g)$ used for exact Levenberg-Marquardt steps is wrong here: it uses $(J^{\mathsf T} J + \mu I) \delta = -g$, which an inexact step does not satisfy, so it would give a wrong $\rho$. This is why line L10 computes the reduction directly.

**Proof of (d).** A step is accepted only if $\rho > 0$, that is $F(x \oplus \delta) < F(x)$ because $\operatorname{pred} > 0$. So accepted costs decrease strictly.

Let $g \ne 0$. Each rejection multiplies $\mu$ by at least $2$, so $\mu \to \infty$ along a run of rejections. From $m_\mu(\delta) \le 0$ we get $\tfrac12 \mu \|\delta\|^2 \le \tfrac12 \delta^{\mathsf T} (J^{\mathsf T} J + \mu I) \delta \le -g^{\mathsf T} \delta \le \|g\| \|\delta\|$, so $\|\delta\| \le 2 \|g\| / \mu \to 0$. The function $\phi(\delta) = \tfrac12 \|r(x \oplus \delta)\|^2$ is twice continuously differentiable near $0$ (for points in front of the cameras), with $\phi(0) = F$, $\nabla \phi(0) = g$. So there is a constant $C$ with $|\phi(\delta) - F - g^{\mathsf T} \delta| \le C \|\delta\|^2$ for small $\delta$, and hence
$$|F(x) - F(x \oplus \delta) - \operatorname{pred}| = |\phi(\delta) - F - g^{\mathsf T} \delta - \tfrac12 \|J \delta\|^2| \le (C + \tfrac12 \|J\|^2) \|\delta\|^2.$$
By (c), $\operatorname{pred} \ge \tfrac12 \mu \|\delta\|^2$. Dividing, $|\rho - 1| \le (2C + \|J\|^2) / \mu \to 0$. So after finitely many rejections $\rho > 0$ and the step is accepted. $\square$

**Remarks.** The argument of (c) and (d) uses only $m_\mu(\delta) \le 0$, $\delta \ne 0$ and the exact back-substitution, not the size of the CG residual. So the forcing parameter $\eta$ and the cap on the CG iterations only affect the speed, never the safety. The speed is a separate question: for Newton's method on a system of equations, Dembo, Eisenstat and Steihaug (1982) prove local linear convergence when the relative residual stays below a constant $\eta_{\max} < 1$, and superlinear convergence when it tends to $0$. The same rates for Levenberg-Marquardt with $\mu$ and $\eta$ both tending to $0$ need further conditions, which this proof does not cover.
