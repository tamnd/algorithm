---
title: The train algorithm frees only garbage, and frees all of it
proves: [correctness]
status: full
---

**Setting.** The mature space is a set of trains, each a sequence of cars, and each car holds objects. Trains get increasing numbers when they are made, and a car has the key $(\text{train}, \text{car number})$, with car numbers increasing in the order the cars are made. The *lowest* train is the one with the smallest number, and $c$ is its first car. The roots include every reference from outside the mature space. The collector also holds a set $X$ of *extra roots*, each a pair $(x, u)$ of an object and a train or the mark "none". Between steps the mutator can allocate (into the last train, or into a new train if the last train is the lowest), store with WRITE, load, and drop roots. A slot $(s, i)$ is *backward* if the car of $s.f_i$ has a smaller key than the car of $s$.

**Invariant (R).** Between steps, every backward slot $(s, i)$ is in the remembered set of the car of $s.f_i$.

*Proof.* At the start there are no objects. A mutator store runs RECORD (line L16), which adds the slot if it is backward. Allocation adds an object whose fields are empty. A load or a dropped root changes no slot. Now take a step that does not end at line L3. Only objects of $c$ move, so the slots whose state can change are the slots of moved objects and the slots that point to moved objects. Line L9 runs RECORD on every field of every moved object. A slot $(p, i)$ that points to a moved object $x$ either has $p$ in $c$, and then $p$ is moved (and line L9 records it) or freed, or has $p$ outside $c$. In the latter case the car of $p$ comes after $c$, since $c$ is the lowest car, so the slot was backward before the step and is in the remembered set of $c$ by (R). The step records every such slot again, at their new targets. All other slots keep their source and target cars. The remembered set of $c$ is dropped with $c$ at line L10, and no surviving object is left in $c$. A step that ends at line L3 frees all of $t$; slots from outside $t$ into $t$ do not exist (see below), and other slots are unchanged. $\square$

Entries can be stale: the slot may have been overwritten. The collector reads only entries whose slot still points into the car, so stale entries do no harm.

**No dangling slots.** After a step, no slot of a surviving object points to a freed object. At line L3: a slot from outside $t$ into $t$ would be backward, so it would be an outside reference, which line L2 rules out; slots inside $t$ are freed with their objects. At line L10: let $z$ be left in $c$ and let $(p, i)$ point to $z$ with $p$ outside $c$. Then the slot is backward, so it is in the remembered set of $c$ by (R), and line L5 or L6 moves $z$, a contradiction. If $p$ is in $c$ and moved, line L8 moves $z$, a contradiction. So only objects that are freed point to $z$.

**Safety.** No reachable object is freed.

*Line L3.* Let $z$ in $t$ be reachable, by a path from a root. Let $y$ be the first object of the path that lies in $t$. Its predecessor is a root, which is an outside reference into $t$, or an object $p$ outside $t$. As $t$ is the lowest train, $p$ is in a later train, so the slot is backward and in a remembered set of a car of $t$ by (R), and its source is in another train. So line L2 finds an outside reference, and line L3 does not run.

*Line L10.* Let $z$ in $c$ be reachable, by a path $r, y_1, \ldots, y_m = z$ from a root $r$. We show by induction along the path that every $y_j$ in $c$ is moved before line L10. Take $y_j$ in $c$. If $j = 1$, a root points to it and line L4 moves it. Otherwise let $p = y_{j-1}$. If $p$ is outside $c$, its car comes after $c$, so the slot is in the remembered set of $c$; if $p$ is in another train line L5 moves $y_j$, and if $p$ is in $t$ line L6 moves it. If $p$ is in $c$, then $p$ is moved by the induction hypothesis, so lines L7 and L8 scan it, since the loop of line L7 visits every moved object, including those moved during the loop, and line L8 moves $y_j$ if it is still in $c$. Line L13 moves each object at most once. Extra roots only make line L4 move more objects. So every reachable object of $c$ leaves $c$, and line L10 frees only unreachable objects. $\square$

Moving an object changes only its car, not its fields or its identity, so the object graph of the reachable objects is unchanged.

**Completeness.** Assume that steps keep running. Every object that is garbage at some time is freed after finitely many steps.

*Claim 1. No object enters the lowest train $t$ from outside it.* Allocation never places into the lowest train. Trains made later have higher numbers, so $t$ stays lowest while it exists. Line L4 moves to a later train or to the train $u$ of an extra root, and $u$ is the train of a slot outside $t$, so $u \ne t$. Line L5 moves to another train. Lines L6 and L8 move into $t$ only objects of $c$, which are already in $t$. So the number of objects of $t$ never grows.

*Claim 2.* Call a step *futile* if it frees nothing and $t$ keeps all its objects. Every step that is not futile reduces the number of objects of $t$ by at least one, since freed objects are in $t$.

*Claim 3. There is no infinite run of futile steps.* Extra roots are dropped at every step that is not futile (line L12, and with the train at line L3). So the first futile step of a run starts with $X$ empty. Line L2 found an outside reference into $t$, and a futile step moves no object out of $t$, so an outside reference into $t$ still exists after it, and line L11 makes its target $x$ an extra root. Then $x$ is not in $c$, which is freed by line L10, but in some later car $d$ of $t$. Futile steps free the lowest car and put the objects of that car into the last car of $t$ or into new cars at its end, so the cars before $d$ are never added to, and each futile step removes one of them. Objects outside the lowest car do not move, and $x$ is held by $X$. So after a number of futile steps no larger than the number of cars before $d$, $d$ is the lowest car, and line L4 moves $x$ to a train other than $t$. That step is not futile.

*Claim 4. The lowest train is freed after finitely many steps.* By claims 1 to 3, the number of objects of $t$ falls by at least one in each bounded run of steps. When it reaches zero, $t$ is gone (a car is made only to hold an object, and an empty train is deleted).

*Claim 5. Garbage is freed.* Let $D$ be the set of objects that are garbage at time $\tau$. They stay garbage. Any slot that points to an object of $D$ after $\tau$ has a source in $D$: the mutator writes only into reachable objects and only reachable values, and new objects start empty, so such a slot already held the same value at $\tau$, and its source could not be reachable at $\tau$. No root points into $D$ after $\tau$. By claim 4 a step that is not futile happens after $\tau$; let $\tau'$ be the end of the first one, so that $X$ is empty at $\tau'$. Let $B$ be the highest train number that holds an object of $D$ at $\tau'$.

We show that after $\tau'$ every object of $D$ lies in a train numbered at most $B$. Consider a move of $x \in D$. Line L4 with a root is impossible, since no root points into $D$. Line L4 with an extra root $(x, u)$: the extra root was made after $\tau'$, from an outside reference to $x$. That reference was not a root, so it was a slot of a source $s$, which is in $D$, and $u$ was the train of $s$ at that time, so $u \le B$ by induction. Line L5: the source of the slot is in $D$ and lies in $u$, so $u \le B$. Line L6: $x$ stays in $t \le B$. Line L8: the source $o$ is in $D$ and lies in the destination train. In each case the new train is at most $B$. Trains made after $\tau'$ have numbers above $B$, so they never hold objects of $D$.

By claim 4, the trains numbered at most $B$ are each freed after finitely many steps, and no new train with such a number is made. When they are all gone, no object of $D$ can exist. $\blacksquare$

**Why the repair is needed.** Without line L11, claim 3 fails: a mutator can keep a root on an object of $t$ that is never in $c$, by loading along a cycle in $t$ before each step. Then each step moves the objects of $c$ to the end of $t$, which is futile, and the trains behind $t$ are never collected. The tests run this mutator, with and without the repair.
