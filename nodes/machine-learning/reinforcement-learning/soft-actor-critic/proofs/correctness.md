---
title: Soft policy iteration converges, and what each SAC update computes
proves: [correctness]
status: full
---

Fix a finite MDP with states $S$, actions $A$, bounded rewards $r(s, a)$, transition probabilities $P(s' \mid s, a)$ whose rows sum to at most 1, $0 \le \gamma < 1$ and $\alpha > 0$. Let $\Pi_+$ be the set of policies with $\pi(a \mid s) > 0$ for all $s, a$. For $\pi \in \Pi_+$ write $V_Q^\pi(s) = \sum_a \pi(a \mid s)(Q(s, a) - \alpha \log \pi(a \mid s))$ and $(T^\pi Q)(s, a) = r(s, a) + \gamma \sum_{s'} P(s' \mid s, a) V_Q^\pi(s')$. All inequalities between functions hold at every argument. For a function $q$ on $A$, let $g_q(a) = e^{q(a)/\alpha} / Z_q$ with $Z_q = \sum_b e^{q(b)/\alpha}$.

**Lemma 0 (Gibbs).** For every distribution $p$ on $A$ with full support, $\sum_a p(a)(q(a) - \alpha \log p(a)) = \alpha \log Z_q - \alpha\, \mathrm{KL}(p \,\|\, g_q)$. Hence the left side is at most $\alpha \log Z_q$, with equality exactly at $p = g_q$.

*Proof.* Since $q(a) = \alpha \log g_q(a) + \alpha \log Z_q$, the left side is $\alpha \log Z_q + \alpha \sum_a p(a) (\log g_q(a) - \log p(a)) = \alpha \log Z_q - \alpha \mathrm{KL}(p \,\|\, g_q)$. The KL divergence is nonnegative and zero only when $p = g_q$. $\square$

**Claim 1 (soft policy evaluation, Lemma 1 of the paper).** For $\pi \in \Pi_+$, $T^\pi$ is monotone and $\|T^\pi Q - T^\pi Q'\|_\infty \le \gamma \|Q - Q'\|_\infty$. So $T^\pi$ has a unique fixed point $Q^\pi$, the iterates $(T^\pi)^k Q_0$ converge to it from any $Q_0$, and $Q^\pi$ is the soft value of $\pi$.

*Proof.* $T^\pi Q - T^\pi Q' = \gamma \sum_{s'} P(s' \mid s, a) \sum_{a'} \pi(a' \mid s')(Q - Q')(s', a')$, because the entropy terms cancel. This is a nonnegative combination of values of $Q - Q'$ with total weight at most $\gamma$, which gives both monotonicity and the bound. Banach's fixed point theorem gives the rest. Unrolling $Q^\pi = T^\pi Q^\pi$ shows that $Q^\pi(s, a)$ is the expected discounted sum of the rewards $r(s_t, a_t)$ plus $\alpha$ times the entropies at the states after the first, which is the soft action value. $\square$

**Claim 2 (soft policy improvement, Lemma 2 of the paper).** Let $\Pi \subseteq \Pi_+$ contain $\pi_{\text{old}}$, let $Q_{\text{old}} = Q^{\pi_{\text{old}}}$, and let $\pi_{\text{new}} \in \Pi$ satisfy $\mathrm{KL}(\pi_{\text{new}}(\cdot \mid s) \,\|\, g_{Q_{\text{old}}(s, \cdot)}) \le \mathrm{KL}(\pi_{\text{old}}(\cdot \mid s) \,\|\, g_{Q_{\text{old}}(s, \cdot)})$ for every $s$ (in particular, any minimizer over $\Pi$). Then $Q^{\pi_{\text{new}}} \ge Q_{\text{old}}$.

*Proof.* By Lemma 0 in each state, $V_{Q_{\text{old}}}^{\pi_{\text{new}}}(s) \ge V_{Q_{\text{old}}}^{\pi_{\text{old}}}(s)$. Hence $T^{\pi_{\text{new}}} Q_{\text{old}} \ge T^{\pi_{\text{old}}} Q_{\text{old}} = Q_{\text{old}}$. Applying the monotone map $T^{\pi_{\text{new}}}$ repeatedly gives $(T^{\pi_{\text{new}}})^k Q_{\text{old}} \ge Q_{\text{old}}$ for every $k$, and the left side tends to $Q^{\pi_{\text{new}}}$ by Claim 1. $\square$

**Claim 3 (soft policy iteration, Theorem 1 of the paper, with $\Pi = \Pi_+$).** Let $\pi_0 \in \Pi_+$ and $\pi_{k+1}(\cdot \mid s) = g_{Q^{\pi_k}(s, \cdot)}$. Then $Q^{\pi_k}$ increases to a limit $Q_\infty$, $\pi_k \to \pi_\infty = g_{Q_\infty}$, $Q_\infty = Q^{\pi_\infty}$, and $Q^\pi \le Q^{\pi_\infty}$ for every $\pi \in \Pi_+$.

*Proof.* $g_q$ has full support, so every $\pi_k \in \Pi_+$, and $\pi_{k+1}$ minimizes the KL divergence (to zero), so Claim 2 gives $Q^{\pi_{k+1}} \ge Q^{\pi_k}$. Each soft value is bounded: rewards are bounded and each entropy is at most $\log |A|$, so $|Q^\pi| \le (\max |r| + \alpha \log |A|)/(1 - \gamma)$. A bounded increasing sequence in the finite-dimensional space $\mathbb{R}^{S \times A}$ converges; call the limit $Q_\infty$. The map $q \mapsto g_q$ is continuous, so $\pi_{k+1} \to \pi_\infty = g_{Q_\infty} \in \Pi_+$. On $\Pi_+$, $Q^\pi$ is the solution of a linear system whose coefficients ($\pi$ and $\pi \log \pi$) depend continuously on $\pi$ and whose matrix stays invertible (Claim 1), so $Q^\pi$ is continuous in $\pi$; thus $Q^{\pi_{k+1}} \to Q^{\pi_\infty}$, and $Q^{\pi_\infty} = Q_\infty$.

Let $T^*$ be the soft optimality operator, $(T^* Q)(s, a) = r(s, a) + \gamma \sum_{s'} P(s' \mid s, a)\, \alpha \log Z_{Q(s', \cdot)}$. By Lemma 0, $T^\pi Q \le T^* Q$ for every $\pi$, with equality when $\pi = g_Q$. The log-sum-exp is monotone and $\alpha \log Z_{q + c} = \alpha \log Z_q + c$ for constants $c$, so $T^*$ is monotone and a $\gamma$-contraction; let $Q^*$ be its fixed point. Since $\pi_\infty = g_{Q_\infty}$ and $Q_\infty = T^{\pi_\infty} Q_\infty = T^* Q_\infty$, we get $Q_\infty = Q^*$. For any $\pi \in \Pi_+$, $Q^\pi = T^\pi Q^\pi \le T^* Q^\pi$, and by monotonicity $Q^\pi \le (T^*)^k Q^\pi \to Q^*$. $\square$

**Claim 4 (reparameterized actor step).** Fix the critics and a state $s$, and let $a_\phi(s, \xi) = \tanh(\mu_\phi(s) + \sigma_\phi(s) \xi)$ (or without tanh), with $\mu_\phi, \sigma_\phi$ differentiable and $\xi \sim N(0, I)$. Let $\bar Q = \min_j Q_{w_j}$. Then, where differentiation and expectation may be exchanged, $\nabla_\phi \mathbb{E}_{a \sim \pi_\phi}[\alpha \log \pi_\phi(a \mid s) - \bar Q(s, a)] = \mathbb{E}_\xi[\nabla_\phi (\alpha \log \pi_\phi(a_\phi(s, \xi) \mid s) - \bar Q(s, a_\phi(s, \xi)))]$, so the minibatch average of line L8 is an unbiased estimate of the gradient of the mean over replayed states. The objective equals $\alpha\, \mathrm{KL}(\pi_\phi(\cdot \mid s) \,\|\, e^{\bar Q(s, \cdot)/\alpha} / Z(s)) - \alpha \log Z(s)$ whenever $Z(s) = \int e^{\bar Q(s, a)/\alpha} da$ is finite, so line L8 descends the KL projection of Claim 2.

*Proof.* The law of $a_\phi(s, \xi)$ is $\pi_\phi(\cdot \mid s)$, so the expectation over $a$ equals the expectation over $\xi$ of the same integrand at $a_\phi(s, \xi)$; the law of $\xi$ does not depend on $\phi$, so the gradient moves inside under the stated exchange condition. The density of $u = \mu + \sigma \xi$ is $N(u; \mu, \sigma^2)$, and $a = \tanh(u)$ is a smooth bijection with $da/du = 1 - a^2$ in each coordinate, so the change of variables gives $\log \pi(a \mid s) = \log N(u; \mu, \sigma^2) - \sum_i \log(1 - a_i^2)$, the formula of the page. The KL identity is the continuous form of Lemma 0, with the same algebra. Averaging $M$ unbiased terms keeps the estimate unbiased. $\square$

**Claim 5 (temperature step).** Let $J(\alpha) = \mathbb{E}[-\alpha \log \pi(a \mid s) - \alpha \bar{\mathcal H}]$ for the current policy. Then $J'(\alpha) = \mathcal{H} - \bar{\mathcal H}$, where $\mathcal{H} = \mathbb{E}[-\log \pi(a \mid s)]$, and the average in line L9 is an unbiased estimate of it. So a gradient step lowers $\alpha$ when the entropy exceeds the target and raises it when the entropy is below the target. The same sign holds for a step on $\log \alpha$ with gradient $\mathcal{H} - \bar{\mathcal H}$.

*Proof.* $J$ is linear in $\alpha$ with slope $\mathbb{E}[-\log \pi] - \bar{\mathcal H}$. The minibatch terms $-\log \pi_\phi(\tilde a_i \mid s_i) - \bar{\mathcal H}$ have this mean when the states follow the replay law and $\tilde a_i \sim \pi_\phi(\cdot \mid s_i)$. Gradient descent moves $\alpha$ against the sign of the slope. $\square$

Claims 1 to 3 prove that tabular soft policy iteration converges to the soft optimal policy, and Claims 4 and 5, together with the regression of the critics on the soft target of line L6 (an unbiased sample of $T^{\pi_\phi}$ applied to the target critic minimum), show that SAC replaces each exact step by an unbiased stochastic gradient step. $\blacksquare$
